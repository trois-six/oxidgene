This guide walks through the application as you meet it: installing it,
creating or importing a tree, then each page in turn. The screenshots show
the Landrevel family, a fictitious family invented for the demonstration.

For step-by-step walkthroughs of whole tasks, such as moving from another
program or recording an ancestor found in the archives, see the
[workflows](@/workflows/_index.md).
