# Credits — screenshot fixture media

The fictitious family of `e2e/screenshots/family.ts`, which `just screenshots`
imports to take the README and specification screenshots, is illustrated with
the public-domain images below. The sitters of the portraits are anonymous:
each source record describes its subject only as an "unknown man" or "unknown
woman" ("onbekende man", "onbekende vrouw"). They are cast as fictitious
persons; no name, date or relationship of the fixture belongs to them.

No image here is AI-generated, shows a living person, or comes from a user's
data. Each was cropped from the mount to the sitter's head and shoulders,
resized (portraits to 300 × 400 pixels, documents to at most 1000 pixels),
and re-encoded as JPEG without metadata.

## Portraits

Cabinet cards from the photograph collection of the Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam.
The museum records each as public domain ("Publiek domein") and releases its
reproduction under the [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
public-domain dedication. The photographer is the studio printed on the mount,
as the museum records it.
